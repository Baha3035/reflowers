# Gül Bishkek

Create a modern, highly user-friendly, mobile-first marketplace website for buying and reselling flowers in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan.

The platform should combine:

Flower bouquets sold by private individuals.

New bouquets sold by professional flower shops.

Discounted flowers that need to be sold quickly.

Local delivery and self-pickup options.

The main purpose of the platform is to make buying and selling flowers in Bishkek easier, faster, safer, and more convenient than using Telegram groups or Instagram pages.

For now, create a polished clickable demo website. Do not build a real backend yet. Use realistic sample data, placeholder users, flower shops, listings, reviews, delivery prices, and locations in Bishkek.

The website must feel premium, warm, emotional, modern, trustworthy, and very easy to use.

Product concept

The platform is a flower marketplace where users can:

sell a bouquet they received but do not need;

buy affordable flowers from other people;

order new bouquets from verified flower shops;

find discounted flowers;

arrange delivery or self-pickup;

communicate with sellers;

save bouquets to favourites;

see freshness, location, reviews, and seller information.

The core message of the product is:

“Beautiful flowers should not go to waste.”

The secondary message is:

“Buy, sell, and deliver flowers in one place.”

Target audience

The primary target audience is:

young people in Bishkek;

women who receive flowers and want to resell them;

people who want to buy beautiful flowers at a lower price;

people buying flowers for birthdays, dates, apologies, celebrations, weddings, and gifts;

flower shops that want an additional sales channel;

users who need same-day flower delivery.
